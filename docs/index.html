I am a first-year PhD candidate in Industrial Economics at the University of Valencia. My research lies at the intersection of labour economics and the economics of education, with a particular focus on skill mismatch, using OECD data. Methodologically, I am interested in applied microeconometrics and causal inference, as well as in how machine learning tools can support empirical work in economics.

Research interests

  • Causal inference
  • Applied microeconometrics
  • Labour economics
  • Economics of education
  • Skill mismatch
  • Machine learning
