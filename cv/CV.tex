\section{Profile}

I am a first-year PhD student in Industrial Economics at the University of Valencia, with a strong academic background and a keen interest in econometrics and quantitative methods.
My research focuses on the economics of skill mismatch, exploring its implications for labor market performance and workers' well-being.
During my academic career, I have gained valuable experience as a Research Assistant in the Department of Economic Structure through a collaboration grant, and later as a Senior Research Technician in the Department of Economic Analysis, both at the University of Valencia.
